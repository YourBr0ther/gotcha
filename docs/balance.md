## Needs

A neutral pokemon (every multiplier 1), awake and left alone from full.

| Need | Base decay /h | 100 to 20 at base rate | 100 to 20 simulated, untouched | Means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hunger | 6.5 | 12.3 h | 12.3 h | feed about twice a day |
| energy | 5 | 16.0 h | 16.0 h | sleep every night |
| hygiene | 3 | 26.7 h | 16.7 h | groom about once a day |
| fun | 8 | 10.0 h | 10.0 h | play a couple of times a day |
| affection | 4 | 20.0 h | 20.1 h | pet or talk once a day |
| health | 0 | never | 24.5 h | only falls while hungry, dirty or sick |

- A full night of sleep (0 to 100 energy) takes 8.3 h, during which hunger falls 27 points.
- Messes every 8.0 h while awake knock hygiene down 15, which is why the simulated hygiene time is shorter than the base rate.
- Left alone from full, the pokemon falls asleep from exhaustion at 20.0 h and gets sick at 21.4 h.
- Health drains 4/h while hunger is under 20 and 3/h while hygiene is under 20; sickness starts when health reaches 50 that way, and clears with medicine or 8.0 h of unbroken sleep on a fed, clean pokemon.
